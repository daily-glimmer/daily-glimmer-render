Are flamingos born pink?
Myth! Flamingo chicks hatch fluffy and gray or white.
Their pink comes from pigments called carotenoids, found in algae and tiny brine shrimp they eat.
Those pigments slowly build up in new feathers, so the pink takes a few years to show.
Without that food, a flamingo's feathers can fade back to pale pink or white.
So a flamingo really is what it eats! What color would you be?
