Do snowshoe hares really turn white in winter?
True! But their fur doesn't change color, they grow a brand new white coat.
The signal is the shorter days of autumn, not the snow or the cold.
The switch takes about six weeks, and in spring it runs backwards to brown.
Their huge furry hind feet work like snowshoes, so they don't sink into deep snow.
White fur on white snow makes them really hard to spot! Could you find one?
