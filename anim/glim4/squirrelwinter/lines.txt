Do squirrels sleep through the whole winter?
Myth! Tree squirrels like the gray squirrel never hibernate.
They stay active all winter and dig up the food they buried in fall.
One squirrel can hide several thousand snacks in a single season, using memory and smell to find them.
On freezing days, they curl up together in cozy nests or tree holes to save heat.
Only ground squirrels truly hibernate! Have you seen a squirrel in the snow?
