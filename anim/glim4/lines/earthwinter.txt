Is it winter because Earth is farther from the Sun?
Myth! Earth is actually closest to the Sun in early January.
Seasons happen because Earth is tilted by about twenty three and a half degrees.
In December, the north leans away from the Sun, so sunlight hits it at a low, weak angle.
Days get shorter too, and that is exactly when Australia has summer!
