How do crickets chirp?
They don't use their voice. They play their wings like a tiny violin!
One wing has a sharp edge called a scraper, and the other has a row of little ridges called a file.
When the scraper rubs across the file, the wings buzz and make the chirp.
Only the males chirp, mostly to call females, and crickets hear with ears on their front legs.
Bonus: they chirp faster when it's warmer! Love the sound or can't sleep?
