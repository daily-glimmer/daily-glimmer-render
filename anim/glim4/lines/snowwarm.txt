Can a blanket of snow actually keep animals warm?
True! Snow is a great insulator, full of tiny pockets of trapped air.
Under about fifteen centimeters of snow, it stays close to zero degrees Celsius.
That's true even when the air above drops to twenty degrees below zero or colder!
Mice, voles and shrews dig tunnels in this hidden world, called the subnivean zone.
A cozy snow roof! Did you know mice live under the snow?
