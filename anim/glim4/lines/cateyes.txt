Why do cats' eyes glow in the dark?
Behind the eye, cats have a shiny layer that works like a mirror.
Light that slips past the eye's sensors hits this mirror and bounces back for a second chance.
That bounced light is the glow we see when a flashlight shines at a cat.
Thanks to it, cats can see with about one sixth of the light we need.
We don't have this mirror, so our eyes just look red in photos. Have you seen a cat's eyes glow?
