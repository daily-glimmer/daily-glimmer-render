How do geckos climb walls?
There is no glue on their feet: the secret is millions of tiny hairs called setae.
Each hair splits into hundreds of even tinier tips, so small they touch the wall almost molecule to molecule.
Up close, a weak pull called the van der Waals force makes each tip stick.
Billions of tips together hold the gecko tight, and it peels its toes off to let go.
