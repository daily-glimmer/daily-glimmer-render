Is a starfish really a fish?
Not even close! Starfish have no gills, no fins and no backbone.
They are echinoderms, close cousins of sea urchins and sand dollars.
Instead of blood, they pump seawater through their bodies to move hundreds of tiny tube feet.
They even have a simple eye at the tip of each arm, and many can regrow a lost arm!
That's why scientists prefer the name sea star! Did you know this one?
