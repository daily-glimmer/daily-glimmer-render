Why do flamingos stand on one leg?
Scientists found it's actually easier for them than standing on two!
A flamingo's body sits right above that one leg, so its own weight locks the joints in place.
In tests, even a flamingo body with no muscle power at all stayed balanced like this.
So they can nap on one leg and barely sway, using almost no energy.
Tucking one leg up may also keep them warm. Can you stand on one leg for a minute?
