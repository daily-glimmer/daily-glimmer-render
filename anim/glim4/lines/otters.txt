Why do sea otters hold hands?
When sea otters nap, they float on their backs out on the open ocean.
But wind and currents could slowly carry sleeping otters away from each other.
So they link paws or wrap up in kelp, staying together in groups called rafts.
A raft can hold dozens of otters, and moms keep their pups safe on their bellies.
Bonus: their fur is the thickest of any animal, up to a million hairs per square inch! Who would you hold hands with?
