Is a tomato really a fruit?
True! To scientists, a tomato is a fruit, and even a berry.
A fruit grows from a flower and holds the seeds, and every tomato is full of them.
Cooks call it a vegetable because it tastes savory, not sweet, just like cucumbers.
In 1893, the United States Supreme Court even ruled it a vegetable for taxes!
Fruit in the garden, vegetable in the kitchen! Which do you say?
