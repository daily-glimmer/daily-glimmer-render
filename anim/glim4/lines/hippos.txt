Can hippos swim?
Surprisingly, no! Hippos are far too heavy to float.
Their bodies are so dense that they sink, so they walk or bounce along the river bottom.
They can hold their breath for about five minutes, then push off the ground to come up for air.
They can even sleep underwater and rise to breathe without waking up!
So a hippo doesn't really swim, it moonwalks underwater! Which animal myth should we bust next?
