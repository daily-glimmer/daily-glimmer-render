Do cows really have four stomachs?
Not quite! A cow has one big stomach with four separate rooms.
The biggest room, the rumen, can hold about 150 liters, and it's full of tiny microbes.
Those microbes break down tough grass that our bodies could never digest.
Then cows burp the food back up and chew it again, for up to eight hours a day!
It's called chewing the cud. Which farm animal should we explain next?
