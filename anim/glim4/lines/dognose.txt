Why do dogs have wet noses?
A thin layer of mucus on the nose catches tiny scent particles from the air.
Dogs lick their noses to keep them wet and pass the smells to a scent organ in the mouth.
A cold, wet nose may even help them sense warmth from animals nearby.
And don't worry: a dry nose alone does not mean your dog is sick.
