Is a banana a berry?
Surprisingly, yes! To botanists, a banana is a true berry.
A true berry grows from one flower with one ovary, and keeps its seeds inside soft flesh.
The tiny black dots in the middle of a banana are seeds that never fully grew.
Strawberries break the rule: their seeds sit on the outside, so they're not true berries!
Even grapes and tomatoes count as berries! Which fruit surprised you the most?
