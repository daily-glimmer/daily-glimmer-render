How do wood frogs survive being frozen?
In winter, a wood frog can freeze almost solid, and its heart even stops beating.
As ice forms on its skin, the liver floods the body with sugar.
The sugar works like antifreeze inside the cells, so they don't burst, while the ice stays outside them.
In spring, the frog thaws, its heart starts again, and it hops away!
