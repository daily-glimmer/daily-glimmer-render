Is a whale a fish?
Myth! Whales are mammals, just like dogs, cows and you.
They breathe air with lungs, so they swim up to breathe through a blowhole.
Whale moms feed their babies milk, and whales are warm-blooded, just like us.
Even their tails give it away: whales swim with an up-and-down tail, fish wiggle side to side.
A giant mammal that lives in the sea! Did you think whales were fish?
