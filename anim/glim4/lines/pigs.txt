Do pigs really sweat a lot?
Not at all! Pigs have hardly any working sweat glands.
So on hot days they can overheat quickly, and they need another way to cool down.
That's why pigs love mud! Wet mud cools their skin as it slowly dries.
The mud layer also works like sunscreen and helps keep away biting insects.
