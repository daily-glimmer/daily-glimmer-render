Is it true that a duck's quack doesn't echo?
No! That's a myth, and acoustic scientists tested it.
In 2003, researchers at the University of Salford recorded a duck quacking and studied the sound.
The echo was there, but a quack fades out slowly, so the echo blends right into it.
Also, ducks rarely quack near big walls or cliffs that bounce sound back.
And in mallards, only the females make that loud quack! Did you believe this myth?
