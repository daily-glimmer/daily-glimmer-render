How do cats land on their feet?
It's called the righting reflex, and kittens learn it at just a few weeks old.
First, the inner ear tells the cat which way is up, in a split second.
Then it twists the front half of its body, and the back half follows.
A super flexible spine and no fixed collarbone make the twist possible.
Then legs spread wide to soften the landing, but falls can still hurt cats, so keep windows safe! Does your cat love high places?
