Do camels really spit at people?
Not quite! What flies out is mostly half-digested food from the stomach.
Like cows, camels chew cud, so they can bring food back up from their stomach.
When a camel feels threatened, it mixes that green goo with saliva and sprays it out.
Its stomach has three chambers, so there is plenty of smelly goo ready to go.
So it's less spit and more like throwing up! Would you stand close to a camel?
