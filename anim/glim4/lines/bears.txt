How do bears survive winter without eating?
In fall, bears eat almost nonstop and build up a thick layer of fat.
In their den, the heart slows from about forty beats a minute to as few as eight.
For months they don't eat, drink, pee, or poop, and they burn fat for energy.
Their body even recycles waste into new protein, so their muscles stay strong.
And mother bears give birth to tiny cubs during this winter sleep! Would you like to sleep all winter?
