Why does the Moon change shape?
It doesn't! The Moon is always a round ball, and the Sun always lights up one half.
As the Moon circles Earth, we see different parts of that sunny half.
When it's between us and the Sun, the lit side faces away. That's a new moon.
When it's on the far side of Earth, we see the whole bright face. Full moon!
