Do carrots really help you see in the dark?
Myth! Eating more carrots won't give you night vision.
Carrots have beta-carotene, which your body turns into vitamin A for your eyes.
Too little vitamin A can cause night blindness, but extra carrots won't boost healthy eyes.
The myth spread in World War Two, when Britain hid its new radar by saying pilots ate lots of carrots.
Clever trick, wrong science! Do you still eat your carrots?
