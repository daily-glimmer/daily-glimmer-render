Do snakes really unhinge their jaws?
Myth! A snake's jaw never comes apart, it's just super stretchy.
Its lower jaw has two halves, joined at the chin by an elastic band.
Each side also has an extra joint, so the mouth can open extra wide.
To swallow, the snake moves its jaws one side at a time, walking its mouth over the food.
No unhinging, just a lot of stretch! Did you believe this myth?
