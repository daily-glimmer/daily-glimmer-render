Do sharks die if they stop swimming?
For most sharks, no! Only a few must keep moving.
Nurse sharks can rest on the sea floor and pump water over their gills with their cheek muscles.
Others swim with their mouths open, so water rushes over the gills, and some can switch between both ways.
Only about two dozen of more than five hundred shark species must swim nonstop.
So many sharks can actually take naps! Which ocean myth should we bust next?
