Why do our fingers get wrinkly in water?
Surprise: it's not just the skin soaking up water like a sponge.
After about five minutes, your nerves tell tiny blood vessels in your fingertips to shrink.
The skin on top gets pulled down into little folds, and that makes the wrinkles.
Scientists found that wrinkly fingers can grip wet objects better, like tire treads.
People with damaged finger nerves don't get wrinkles at all! Did you know that?
