Why do male seahorses give birth?
The mom places her eggs into a special pouch on the dad's belly.
He fertilizes them inside and seals the pouch, a bit like a tiny womb.
For about two to four weeks, he gives the babies oxygen and food, and keeps them safe.
Then he pumps out hundreds of tiny babies, sometimes more than a thousand!
Scientists think this lets mom make new eggs sooner. Know any other super dads?
