Do dogs sweat through their tongues?
Myth! A dog's tongue has no sweat glands at all.
Dogs actually sweat a little through their paw pads, so you might spot damp paw prints.
But paw sweat only releases a bit of heat, so dogs mainly cool off by panting.
Fast breathing evaporates moisture from the tongue and airways, and that cools them down.
Sweaty paws and a panting tongue! Does your dog pant a lot?
