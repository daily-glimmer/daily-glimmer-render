Is the North Star the brightest star in the sky?
Myth! The North Star, called Polaris, is not even close to the brightest.
It ranks only around forty eighth in brightness among the stars we see at night.
The brightest is Sirius, which is just eight and a half light-years away from us.
Polaris is famous because it sits almost above the North Pole, so it always stays in the north.
Not the shiniest, but the most steady! Can you find it tonight?
