Why does popcorn pop?
Every kernel has a tiny drop of water locked inside a tough, hard shell.
When you heat it to about 180 degrees Celsius, that water turns into steam.
The steam pushes harder and harder until the shell suddenly bursts open.
The soft starch inside puffs up into a fluffy white foam, turning the kernel inside out.
That pop sound is the steam escaping! Team salty or team sweet popcorn?
