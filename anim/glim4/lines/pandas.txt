Why do pandas eat so much bamboo?
Surprise: pandas have the stomach and gut of a meat eater.
They can digest less than a fifth of the bamboo they eat.
So they munch up to fourteen hours a day, eating twelve to thirty eight kilograms!
A special wrist bone works like a thumb, so they can hold the stalks tight.
