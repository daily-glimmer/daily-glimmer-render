Why do we see lightning before we hear thunder?
A lightning bolt heats the air to five times hotter than the surface of the Sun.
The air expands so fast that it booms, and that boom is thunder.
Light reaches your eyes almost instantly, but sound needs about three seconds per kilometer.
So count the seconds after the flash and divide by three to know how far away it was.
If you can hear thunder, head inside! How many seconds did you count last time?
