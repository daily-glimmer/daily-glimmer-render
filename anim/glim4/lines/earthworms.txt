Do earthworms really become two worms when cut in half?
Sadly, that is mostly a myth!
The front end holds the worm's brain and its five pairs of hearts.
If the cut is far enough back, that front part can sometimes grow a new tail.
But the tail part can't grow a new head, so it usually dies.
Some flatworms can regrow a whole body from a tiny piece, though! Which animal superpower would you want?
