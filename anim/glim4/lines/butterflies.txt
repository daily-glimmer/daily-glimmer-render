Do butterflies really taste with their feet?
True! Their feet are covered in tiny taste sensors.
When a female lands on a plant, she drums on the leaf with her feet to taste it.
That tells her if it's the right plant for her hungry caterpillars to eat.
If it tastes right, she lays her eggs there, then sips nectar with her long, curly tongue.
So their feet work like a second tongue! What would you taste with your feet?
