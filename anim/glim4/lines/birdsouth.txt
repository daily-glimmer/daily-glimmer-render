Do birds fly south just because they get cold?
Not quite! Most migrating birds are chasing food, not warmth.
In winter, insects, nectar and seeds get hard to find up north.
Fluffy feathers keep many birds warm, so birds that can still find food often stay all winter.
Some fly incredibly far: Arctic terns travel about seventy thousand kilometers every year!
It's one giant food trip! Which birds do you see in winter?
