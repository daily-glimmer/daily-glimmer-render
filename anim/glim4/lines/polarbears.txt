How do polar bears stay warm in the Arctic?
Under their fur hides a thick layer of fat, up to 11 centimeters thick.
Two layers of fur trap warm air, like a cozy winter jacket.
Surprise: their hairs are actually see-through, and the skin underneath is black!
Small ears and a short tail also help them keep their heat inside.
