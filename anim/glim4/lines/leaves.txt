Why do leaves change color in fall?
All summer, leaves are packed with green chlorophyll, which turns sunlight into food.
When days get shorter and cooler, trees stop making it, and the green fades away.
The yellow and orange were hiding in the leaf all along, now they finally show!
Reds are different: many trees make them fresh in fall, from sugars trapped in the leaf.
Sunny days and cool nights make the reds even brighter! What is your favorite fall color?
