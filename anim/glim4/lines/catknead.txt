Why do cats knead with their paws?
It starts when they're tiny kittens, drinking their mom's milk.
Kittens press on their mom's belly with their little paws to help the milk flow.
Grown-up cats keep this habit when they feel safe, cozy and happy.
Their paw pads also have scent glands, so kneading quietly marks you as theirs!
So if your cat makes biscuits on you, that's a big compliment. Does your cat knead?
