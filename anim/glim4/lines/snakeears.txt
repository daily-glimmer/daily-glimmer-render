Can snakes hear without ears?
True! Snakes have no outer ears, but they still have inner ears.
A tiny bone links the inner ear to the jaw, so the jaw picks up vibrations.
Snakes feel footsteps through the ground, and some can sense deep sounds in the air.
A study of royal pythons found they hear best at low pitches, around eighty to one hundred sixty hertz.
They listen with their whole jaw! Did you know snakes could hear?
