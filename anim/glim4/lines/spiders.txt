Is a spider an insect?
Myth! Spiders are arachnids, a completely different group of animals.
Insects have six legs, but spiders always have eight.
An insect's body has three main parts, but a spider's body has only two.
Spiders also have no antennae and no wings, while many insects have both.
Scorpions and ticks are arachnids too! Did you think spiders were insects?
