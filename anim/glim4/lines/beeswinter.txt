Do honeybees sleep through the winter?
Myth! Honeybees stay awake all winter, huddled in a big ball.
They shiver their flight muscles without moving their wings, and that makes heat.
The middle of the cluster stays toasty, roughly twenty seven to thirty five degrees Celsius.
The bees take turns moving between the cold outside and the warm center, and live on stored honey.
A buzzing winter heater made of bees! Did you know they do this?
