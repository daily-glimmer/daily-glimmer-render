Why do stars twinkle?
Starlight travels for years, but its last tiny trip through our air is the bumpy part.
Moving layers of warm and cold air bend the light this way and that.
Stars are so far away that they look like tiny points, so every wobble shows.
Planets look like little disks instead, so they shine more steadily.
Out in space, above the air, stars don't twinkle at all! Have you ever spotted a planet?
