Can a turtle crawl out of its shell?
Myth! A turtle's shell is part of its skeleton.
Its ribs and backbone grow wide and flat, then fuse right into the top of the shell.
Leaving the shell would mean leaving behind its spine, its ribs and about fifty bones.
The shell even has nerves and blood vessels, so a turtle can feel a touch on it.
Its shell is its home and its skeleton! Did you know that?
