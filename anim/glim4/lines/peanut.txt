Is a peanut really a nut?
Myth! A peanut is a legume, a cousin of peas and beans.
Real nuts like hazelnuts grow on trees and have a hard shell around one seed.
Peanuts grow in pods, like peas, but the pods ripen underground!
After the flowers bloom, the stalks push down into the soil, where the peanuts grow.
So a peanut is really more of a bean! Did you know this one?
