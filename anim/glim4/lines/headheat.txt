Do you lose most of your body heat through your head?
Myth! Your head loses only about ten percent of your body heat.
That's roughly its share of your skin, so it loses heat like any other bare part.
The myth came from an old army test where people wore warm suits, but no hats.
Of course the bare head lost the most heat, because everything else was covered!
Still, a hat keeps you cozy! Are you a hat or a hood person?
