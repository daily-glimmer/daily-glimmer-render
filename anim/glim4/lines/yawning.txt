Why do we yawn?
For years people thought it was to get more oxygen, but experiments showed that's not it.
A popular idea today: a big yawn pulls in cool air and fresh blood, which may help cool the brain.
That might help us feel more alert and focused, not just tired and sleepy.
Yawns are also contagious, especially between friends and family.
Even reading about yawning can make you yawn! So be honest: did you just yawn?
