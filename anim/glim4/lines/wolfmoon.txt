Do wolves really howl at the moon?
Myth! Scientists found no link between howling and the moon.
Wolves howl at night because that's when they are most active, and the moon just happens to be up.
A howl calls the pack together and helps a lost wolf find its way home.
It also tells other packs: this territory is taken, stay away!
