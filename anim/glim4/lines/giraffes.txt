How long do giraffes sleep?
Much less than you'd think, and usually in tiny naps.
Most of the time they doze standing up, ready to run from lions.
Zoo studies found about four and a half hours a day, split into many short naps.
For deep sleep, a giraffe lies down and curls its long neck back to rest its head on its body.
That deep sleep lasts only a few minutes at a time! How many hours do you sleep?
