If you touch a baby bird, will its mother reject it?
No! That's a myth. Parent birds don't abandon a chick because it smells like a human.
They react to its begging calls and its wide open beak, so they keep on feeding it.
A tiny chick without feathers can be gently placed back into its nest.
But a fluffy fledgling hopping on the ground is usually fine, because its parents are nearby.
So keep your distance, and let the parents do their job! Have you ever found a baby bird?
