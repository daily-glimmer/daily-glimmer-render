Are zebras white with black stripes?
Not quite! Under the fur, a zebra's skin is black all over.
Pigment cells make dark melanin, and that is what colors the black stripes.
In the white stripes, those cells are switched off, so the hair grows without color.
Scientists think the stripes may help keep biting flies away.
So a zebra is really black with white stripes! Did you guess right?
