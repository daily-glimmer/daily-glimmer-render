Can you see the Great Wall of China from space?
Not with your naked eye! The wall is very long, but only about as wide as a road.
It also has the same colors as the hills around it, so from far above it blends right in.
China's first astronaut, Yang Liwei, looked for it in 2003 and said he could not see it.
With a zoom lens, astronauts can photograph it, and at night city lights are easy to spot.
And from the Moon, no human structure is visible at all! Which space myth should we bust next?
