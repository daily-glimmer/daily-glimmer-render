Why don't spiders get stuck in their own webs?
First, not every thread is sticky. The spokes are dry, and spiders love walking on them.
Their legs are also covered in tiny branching hairs, so only a few tips touch the glue.
On top of that, a special coating on their legs makes the glue grip much less.
And they step very carefully, like dancers on a tightrope.
So bugs get stuck, but the spider walks free! Spiders: cool or creepy?
