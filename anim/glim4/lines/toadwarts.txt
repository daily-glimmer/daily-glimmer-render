Can touching a toad really give you warts?
Myth! Touching a toad can't give you warts at all.
Human warts are caused by a virus called HPV, which spreads between people.
The bumps on a toad's skin aren't warts, they're glands that make a bad-tasting poison.
That poison protects toads from predators, so always wash your hands after touching one.
