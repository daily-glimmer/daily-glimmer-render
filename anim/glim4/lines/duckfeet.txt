Do ducks get frozen feet when they stand on ice?
No! That's a myth. Duck legs have a built-in heat trick.
Warm blood flowing down the legs passes right next to cold blood flowing back up.
The heat jumps across, so the feet stay just above freezing, while the body stays warm.
And duck feet are mostly bone and tendon, with little muscle that needs warmth.
