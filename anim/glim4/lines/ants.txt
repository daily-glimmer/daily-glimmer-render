Can ants really lift fifty times their own weight?
Yes, and some can do even more!
Small animals have a big advantage: their muscles are strong compared to their tiny weight.
In one lab test, an ant's neck held about five thousand times its body weight before it broke.
That tough little neck lets ants carry heavy loads in their jaws.
