Do goats really eat tin cans?
Myth! Goats nibble on cans, but they don't actually eat the metal.
They are curious and test new things with their lips and mouth, a bit like toddlers.
What they really munch on is the paper label and the tasty glue behind it.
Goats are picky eaters that love leaves, twigs and shrubs.
So the tin can stays, and the label disappears! Did you believe this myth?
