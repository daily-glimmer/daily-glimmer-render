What are cat whiskers really for?
Each whisker sits deep in the skin, surrounded by lots of nerves.
They feel the tiniest touch, even small changes in the air around them.
Their whiskers are about as wide as their body, so a cat can test if a gap is big enough.
They show mood, too: whiskers forward means curious, pulled back means scared.
Bonus: cats even have whiskers on the back of their front legs! Did you know that?
