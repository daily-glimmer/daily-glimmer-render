Can a cockroach really live without its head?
True! A headless cockroach can keep going for weeks.
It breathes through tiny holes along its body, not through its head.
Its blood flows at low pressure, and the neck wound quickly seals with a clot.
Little nerve clusters in every body segment still let it stand, walk and react to touch.
In the end it dies because it can't eat or drink! Would you have guessed?
