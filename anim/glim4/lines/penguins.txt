Do penguins really have no knees?
Myth! Every penguin has knees, hidden inside its body.
Under all those feathers, the thigh bone and knee stay tucked up in a deep crouch.
The short legs you see are mostly the ankle and the foot.
Scientists found that walking costs them about twice the energy of other animals their size.
So every penguin is secretly squatting all day long! What surprised you most?
