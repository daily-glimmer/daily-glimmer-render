Why don't fish freeze when a lake turns to ice?
Surprise: the ice on top actually keeps them safe!
Water is heaviest at about four degrees Celsius, so that water sinks to the bottom.
Colder water and ice float on top, and the ice works like a lid that keeps the warmth in.
Fish slow down their bodies and rest near the bottom until spring.
A frozen lake is a cozy winter home! Did you know ice could protect fish?
