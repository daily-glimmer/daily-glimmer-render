Do moths really love flames and bright lights?
Nope! They aren't attracted to light at all. They get trapped by it.
For millions of years, the brightest thing at night was the sky above.
So insects learned to keep their backs turned toward the light, to know which way is up.
Near a lamp, that rule makes them tilt, circle, and even flip upside down.
High-speed cameras proved it in 2024. Have you seen a moth circle your porch light?
