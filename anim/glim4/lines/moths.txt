Do moths eat your clothes?
Not quite! Grown-up clothes moths don't eat anything at all.
In fact, adult clothes moths don't even have working mouthparts.
It's their tiny caterpillars, the larvae, that munch on wool, fur and feathers.
They can digest keratin, a tough protein in animal fibers, which few insects can.
So the hungry ones are the babies! Did you think adult moths were the culprits?
