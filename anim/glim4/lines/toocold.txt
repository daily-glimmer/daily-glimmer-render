Can it really be too cold to snow?
Myth! Snow experts say it can be too warm to snow, but never too cold.
Snowflakes need water vapor, and very cold air can only hold a little of it.
So the coldest days are often dry, and the heaviest snow falls when it's just below zero.
Parts of Antarctica are freezing but so dry that they barely get any snow at all!
Cold is fine, dry is the problem! Do you like snow days?
