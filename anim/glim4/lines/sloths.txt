Why are sloths so slow?
It's all about their food: sloths eat leaves, which give them very little energy.
Digesting one meal can take weeks, inside a big stomach with several chambers.
So sloths save energy everywhere: they have less muscle than similar mammals, and their body temperature changes with the weather.
Being slow even helps them hide: tiny algae grow in their fur and turn it green!
And they climb down to poop only about once a week! Would you like to live at sloth speed?
