Did giraffes get long necks by stretching to reach leaves?
That's a myth: a neck you stretch doesn't get passed on to your babies.
Instead, giraffes born with slightly longer necks reached more food and raised more young.
Over millions of years, necks grew to about six feet, still with just seven bones, like ours.
Males also swing their necks in fights called necking, so longer necks often win.
