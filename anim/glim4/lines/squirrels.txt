How do squirrels find their buried nuts?
In fall, one squirrel can bury thousands of nuts, each one in a different spot.
They don't rely on luck: squirrels remember the places, using trees and rocks as landmarks.
Some even sort their stash, burying the same kind of nut together, like a pantry.
Up close, their strong sense of smell helps them find the exact spot.
And the nuts they forget can grow into new trees! Have you seen a squirrel digging this fall?
