Do sharks have bones?
Myth! A shark's skeleton is made of cartilage, not bone.
Cartilage is the same bendy stuff that's in the tip of your nose and your ears.
It's only about half as dense as bone, so it helps sharks stay light in the water.
A big oily liver keeps them floating, and the cartilage lets them twist and turn fast.
No bones at all, but plenty of teeth! Did you know sharks have no bones?
