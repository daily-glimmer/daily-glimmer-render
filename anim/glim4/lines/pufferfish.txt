How do pufferfish puff up?
When a predator gets close, a pufferfish gulps water into its stomach super fast.
Its stomach and skin are so stretchy, it can swell to about three times its normal size.
Many puffers also have sharp spines, and lots of them carry a strong poison too.
But puffing up is exhausting and stressful, so they only do it in real danger.
Once safe, they spit the water out and shrink back to normal! Which sea animal is next?
