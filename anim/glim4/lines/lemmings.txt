Do lemmings really jump off cliffs on purpose?
No! It is one of the biggest animal myths ever.
Every three or four years, lemming numbers boom, and many set off to find new homes.
They can swim, so they cross rivers and lakes, and sadly a few drown along the way.
The famous cliff scene came from a 1958 nature film, and the filmmakers staged it.
Real lemmings just want space and food! Which animal myth fooled you?
