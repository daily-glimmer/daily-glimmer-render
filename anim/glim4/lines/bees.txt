Do all bees die after they sting?
No! Only honeybee workers do, and only sometimes.
Their stinger has tiny barbs, so it gets stuck in thick skin like ours and tears away from the bee.
Bumblebees and most other bees have smooth stingers, so they can sting again and again.
And male bees can't sting at all!
So most bees survive a sting, but they'd much rather collect nectar. Which bug myth should we bust next?
