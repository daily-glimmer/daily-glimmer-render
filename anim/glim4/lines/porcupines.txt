Can porcupines shoot their quills?
No! A porcupine can't fire its quills even an inch!
It has about thirty thousand quills, and each tip is covered in tiny barbs, like little fishing hooks.
The quills are attached loosely, so they pop off when a curious nose touches them.
Before that, a porcupine warns you: it rattles its quills and swings its tail.
So the best defense is simply no hugging! Would you ever try to give a porcupine a hug?
