Is there a dark side of the Moon?
Not really! The far side gets just as much sunlight as the side we see.
The Moon spins exactly once each time it circles Earth, so the same side always faces us.
That's why we never see the far side from Earth. It's hidden, not dark.
At new moon, when our side looks dark, the far side is in full sunshine!
