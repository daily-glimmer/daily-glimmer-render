How do kangaroos hop so far?
Their huge back legs push off together, sending them up to eight meters in one leap.
The secret is their long tendons, which stretch like springs when they land.
That stored energy bounces them into the next hop, almost for free.
Amazingly, over a wide range of speeds, hopping faster barely costs them extra energy!
And when they move slowly, their strong tail works like a fifth leg! Which animal jumps best?
