Why do we get hiccups?
It starts with your diaphragm, the big breathing muscle under your lungs.
Sometimes it gets irritated, for example when you eat too fast or drink fizzy soda, and then it twitches.
Each little twitch pulls air into your lungs in a sudden gulp.
Your vocal cords snap shut to stop the air, and that makes the hic sound.
