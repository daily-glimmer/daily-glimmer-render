Why does your stomach growl?
Your stomach and gut are long tubes lined with muscles that squeeze food along.
That squeezing also pushes gas and liquid around, and that bubbling makes the sound.
When your stomach is empty, strong cleanup waves sweep through every hour or two.
With no food inside to muffle the noise, the rumble gets much louder!
Doctors even have a name for it: borborygmi. Does your stomach growl at the worst moments?
