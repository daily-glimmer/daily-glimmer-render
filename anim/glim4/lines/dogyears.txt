Is one dog year really seven human years?
Myth! Dogs age much faster at the start, and slower later on.
Scientists at UC San Diego compared tiny chemical tags on the DNA of dogs and people.
They found a one-year-old dog is a lot like a thirty-year-old human!
A four-year-old dog matches a person of about fifty two, and then aging slows down.
