How do owls fly so silently?
Most birds make a whooshing sound when they flap, but owls barely make a sound.
The front edge of their wings has tiny comb-like teeth that break up the rushing air.
Their feathers are soft and velvety, so they soak up sound instead of swishing.
Big wings let them glide slowly, with only a few gentle flaps.
