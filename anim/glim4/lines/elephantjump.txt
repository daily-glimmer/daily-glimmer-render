Can elephants really jump?
Myth! Elephants are one of the few mammals that can't jump at all.
Even when they run fast, at least one foot always stays on the ground.
A big elephant can weigh more than five thousand kilograms, far too heavy to launch into the air.
Their straight, pillar-like legs are built to carry weight, not to spring.
Hippos can briefly fly at a run, but elephants never! Did you know that?
