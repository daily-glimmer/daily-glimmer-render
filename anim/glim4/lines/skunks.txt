Why do skunks smell so bad?
Their spray comes from two small glands under the tail.
It's full of sulfur chemicals called thiols, which our noses can sense in tiny amounts.
But skunks warn you first: they hiss, stamp their feet, and raise their tail.
Only then do they spray, hitting targets up to three meters away.
Refilling their supply can take up to ten days, so they don't waste it! Have you ever smelled one?
