Does cracking your knuckles really cause arthritis?
Myth! Studies have found no clear link between knuckle cracking and arthritis.
The pop comes from a tiny gas bubble forming in the fluid inside your joint.
In 2015, scientists filmed it in an MRI scanner and watched the bubble appear.
One doctor cracked only his left hand, twice a day for decades, and neither hand got arthritis.
Pop away, just be gentle! Do you crack your knuckles?
