Why do onions make us cry?
An onion keeps two chemicals apart in its cells, like a tiny trap.
When your knife cuts the cells open, they mix, and an enzyme builds an irritating gas.
That gas floats up into your eyes, and your eyes flush it away with tears.
It's the onion's defense against animals that try to eat it!
Tip: chill the onion first and use a sharp knife for fewer tears. What makes you cry in the kitchen?
