How do dolphins sleep without drowning?
Dolphins have to swim up to the surface to breathe, so they can never fully switch off.
Instead, only one half of their brain sleeps at a time.
The other half stays awake to keep them breathing and watching out for danger.
They even keep one eye open, then swap sides so both halves get their rest.
Imagine sleeping with only half your brain! Would you like to sleep like a dolphin?
