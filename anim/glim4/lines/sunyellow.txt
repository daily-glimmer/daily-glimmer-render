Is the Sun really yellow?
Myth! The Sun is actually white, not yellow.
It shines in every color of the rainbow at once, and together they mix to white.
Astronauts on the space station see a bright white Sun against a pitch black sky.
Down here, our air scatters away some blue light, so the Sun can look yellow, and orange at sunset.
A white star in disguise! Did you think the Sun was yellow?
