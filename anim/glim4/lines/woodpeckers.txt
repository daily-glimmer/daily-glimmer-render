Why don't woodpeckers get headaches?
A woodpecker can hit a tree up to twenty times a second, thousands of times a day.
For years, people thought its skull worked like a soft cushion that soaks up the shock.
But a slow motion study in 2022 found the head works more like a stiff hammer.
The real trick is size: a woodpecker's brain is tiny, and small brains handle these hits far better than big ones.
So it keeps drumming, safe and sound! Which bird should we explain next?
