Is a koala really a bear?
Not at all! Koalas are not bears. They are marsupials, just like kangaroos.
Early European settlers thought they looked like fluffy teddy bears, and the name stuck.
A newborn koala is about the size of a jelly bean, and it crawls straight into its mother's pouch.
This baby, called a joey, grows in the pouch for about six months before peeking out.
Their closest living cousin is actually the wombat! Which animal name fooled you?
