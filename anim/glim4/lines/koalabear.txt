Is a koala a bear?
Myth! A koala is a marsupial, not a bear at all.
Like kangaroos, koala moms carry their tiny babies in a pouch on their belly.
A newborn koala, called a joey, is only the size of a jelly bean.
It grows in the pouch for about six months, then rides around on mom's back.
Early English settlers simply thought they looked like little bears! Did you call it a koala bear?
