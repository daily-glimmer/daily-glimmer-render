Why do frogs croak?
Mostly it's the males, calling to attract a mate and to warn their rivals.
They push air from their lungs past their vocal cords, without even opening their mouth.
A stretchy vocal sac on the throat blows up like a balloon and makes the sound much louder.
Every frog species has its own special call, so females know exactly who is singing.
Some tiny frogs are as loud as a lawn mower! Have you heard frogs at night?
