Why do we get goosebumps?
Every hair on your skin has a tiny muscle attached to it.
When you're cold or excited, nerves tell these muscles to tighten, pulling each hair upright.
In furry animals, that fluffs up the fur to trap warm air, or makes them look bigger.
We lost most of our fur, so now we just see little bumps!
And in mice, scientists found the same nerves also help hair grow! When did you last get goosebumps?
