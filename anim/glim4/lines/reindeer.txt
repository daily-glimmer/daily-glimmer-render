Do reindeer eyes really change color in winter?
True! Their eyes turn from gold in summer to deep blue in winter.
Behind the retina sits a mirror layer called the tapetum, which bounces light back through the eye.
In the dark Arctic winter, their pupils stay wide open for months and eye pressure rises.
That squeezes tiny fibers in the mirror closer together, so it turns blue and keeps more light inside.
Less sharp, but much better in the dark! Would you want winter eyes?
