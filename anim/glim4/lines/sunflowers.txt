Do sunflowers really follow the sun?
Only when they are young! Then they stop.
Young sunflowers turn from east to west during the day, following the sun across the sky.
At night they swing back to face east again, guided by an inner clock.
Grown-up sunflowers stop moving and face east for good, so they warm up fast in the morning.
Warm flowers get about five times more bee visits than cold ones! Did you know this one?
