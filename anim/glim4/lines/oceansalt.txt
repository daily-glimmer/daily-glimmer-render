Why is the ocean salty?
Rain is slightly acidic, so it slowly wears away rocks on land.
Rivers carry those tiny bits of salt and minerals into the sea.
The sun lifts water back into the sky, but the salt stays behind.
Over millions of years, the salt built up to about three and a half percent.
Spread out on land, the ocean's salt would be as tall as a forty story building! Did you know that?
