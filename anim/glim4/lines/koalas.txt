Why do koalas sleep so much?
Koalas can snooze up to twenty hours a day, curled up in the fork of a tree.
Their only food, eucalyptus leaves, is tough, low in energy and full of toxins.
A very long gut and a busy liver work for hours to break the leaves down safely.
So instead of running around, koalas save energy by resting almost all day.
