Does a giraffe have more neck bones than you?
Myth! A giraffe has seven neck bones, exactly like you.
Almost every mammal has seven, from mice to whales.
The difference is size: each giraffe neck bone can be about twenty five centimeters long.
That makes a neck nearly two meters long, held up by strong muscles and ligaments.
Same number of bones, just super-sized! Did you know that?
