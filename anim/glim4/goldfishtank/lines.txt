Does a goldfish only grow to the size of its tank?
Myth! Tank size alone doesn't magically set how big a goldfish gets.
In a small tank, waste builds up in the water, and that dirty water stunts its growth.
Stress and crowding slow it down even more, so the fish stays small but isn't healthier.
With clean water and lots of space, common goldfish can grow up to about thirty centimeters.
So a tiny bowl is not a cozy home! Did you believe this myth?
