Do ducks get cold feet standing on ice?
Not really! Their feet get chilly, but they never freeze.
Warm blood flowing down the leg passes right next to cold blood coming back up.
It hands over its heat, so the feet stay just above freezing and the body stays warm.
In mallards, only about five percent of their body heat escapes through the feet.
A built-in heat exchanger in every leg! Would you stand barefoot on ice?
