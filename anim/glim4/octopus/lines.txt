Does an octopus really have three hearts?
True! Two small hearts pump blood through the gills, and one big heart feeds the body.
Their blood is blue, because it carries oxygen with copper instead of iron.
Copper blood works well in cold water with very little oxygen.
But when an octopus swims, its main heart stops beating, so it prefers to crawl.
