Why can you see your breath when it's cold?
Surprise: what you see isn't air, it's a tiny cloud of water!
The air you breathe out is warm and full of invisible water vapor.
When it hits cold air, it cools down fast and passes its dew point.
The vapor turns into millions of tiny droplets, the same way a real cloud forms.
So every cold breath makes a mini cloud! Did you know you could make clouds?
