Do crocodiles really cry while they eat?
True! Scientists filmed alligators and caimans, and many teared up during meals.
In one study, five out of seven animals had tears in their eyes while eating.
Some eyes even frothed and bubbled with tears!
Researchers think hissing and huffing while eating pushes air through their sinuses and squeezes out tears.
But they're not sad, just eating! Did you think crocodile tears were real?
